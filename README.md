# Hoyeon Lee

**AI Research Scientist · Tech Lead at NAVER Cloud**

I am a Senior AI Research Scientist and Tech Lead in the Voice team at NAVER Cloud. My research focuses on **Speech-Language & Multimodal AI** and **Large Language Models**, with the goal of building AI systems that understand, reason, and interact across speech, language, and context.

I work on language and speech technologies for real-world AI products, spanning pronunciation modeling, phrase break prediction, data generation, and model evaluation. Recently, I have been exploring how LLMs can extend these systems through multimodal reasoning, tool use, and scalable agentic applications.

I connect research and engineering through technical leadership, mentoring, and production deployment. Since 2026, I have also served as an AI Technical Mentor at **AI·SW Maestro**.
